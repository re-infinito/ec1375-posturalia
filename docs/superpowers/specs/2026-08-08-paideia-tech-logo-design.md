# Paideia Tech — Logo Design

## Objective

Create a minimal, official-looking identity for Paideia Tech: the digital EC1375 certification platform for alternative therapists in Mexico. The mark must convey trust, formal professional development, and calm rather than wellness or spa culture.

## Selected Direction: Portal of Knowledge

The selected symbol is an abstract, symmetrical portal built from two cyan architectural planes. It can also read as an open book or a structured path. A short gold horizontal bar completes the lower opening as the earned certification level.

This avoids literal health, massage, acupuncture, and generic app-checkmark imagery while retaining a clear association with education, access, legitimacy, and professional advancement.

## Design System

- Background: `#050A1A`.
- Structural symbol and primary wordmark: `#0088FF` / white.
- Achievement accent: `#FFD700` (use sparingly; one structural stroke only).
- Supporting text: `#D0D0D0`.
- Wordmark: geometric humanist sans serif, medium weight for `Paideia`; lighter and smaller `Tech` descriptor.
- Construction: squared line endings and a symmetrical 90-degree grid; no gradients, shadows, medical motifs, wellness motifs, or script typography.

## Deliverables

1. Horizontal dark-background logo: cyan/gold symbol, white `Paideia`, subdued `Tech`.
2. Square app/favicon symbol: portal only, cyan/gold on transparent background.
3. One-color white and one-color cyan variants for constrained placements.
4. SVG source files, plus transparent PNG exports suitable for existing static HTML pages.
5. A small usage note covering clear space, minimum sizes, and forbidden treatments.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Legible at 24 px high in a header and recognisable as a 16–32 px favicon.
- Strong contrast on the site navy background.
- No literal therapeutic, spa, medical, government-seal, or generic-checkmark iconography.
- The symbol remains distinct without the wordmark.
- All final assets are production-ready and located in `assets/images/logo/`.
